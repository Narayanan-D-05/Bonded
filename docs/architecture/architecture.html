✉️ Vendor invoice / email

Claims the vendor, payout address and amount. May be spoofed: "we changed our bank account" (BEC).

🤖 AI accounts-payable agent

Reads the invoice and proposes a payment. It never moves money itself: every proposal goes through Bonded.

SDK enforce() · MCP tool

Vendor portal

A real vendor changing bank details must prove they are a unique human with World ID (IDKit, verified server-side).

Only then is the new address written to the vendor master, and it still counts as a recent change.

CLEARED Paid automatically

Every re-derived fact matches the invoice.

settle() consumes the on-chain Verdict object and moves USDSUI from the vault to the vendor.

HELD Waits for a verified human

Only the genuinely ambiguous case: recent bank change or a large irreversible payment.

World ID for Agents step-up: a fresh human verification, valid for 5 minutes.
settle_with_stepup() pays only with that proof.

REFUSED Nothing is paid

A fact doesn't match or the address is risky. The refusal shows the claimed value next to the real one, never a bare "no".

4 · Proof & integration

On-chain audit trail

Policy hash, Verdict objects and every USDSUI payment live on Sui testnet, linkable on Suiscan.

Screening evidence

Each Intercepta response is stored and referenced by its sha256 hash, so a refusal can be audited later.

Bonded console

Invoice inbox, premise-diff review, vendor portal and activity. People only open the held invoices.
